Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Summary

ster0mode develops the neur0tide iOS app. neur0tide does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Preferences and optional session feedback stay on your iPhone. The app does not use the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Selected wave, session duration, and safety disclaimer acceptance
  • Optional post-session feedback (stored locally to support future personalization)

This information is stored using iOS User Defaults on your device only. It is not transmitted to us or any third party.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No networking or third-party SDKs
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, or health data

Audio

neur0tide generates audio on your device for headphone-based listening. It is a general wellness app, not a medical device, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or monitor any disease or medical condition.

Children

neur0tide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@neur0tide.com
