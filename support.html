Support

Help with the neur0tide iOS app.

Before you start

  • Use headphones at a comfortable volume
  • Do not use while driving or operating machinery
  • Stop if you feel discomfort, dizziness, anxiety, nausea, or headache
  • If you have epilepsy, seizures, serious mental-health concerns, a neurological condition, or implanted medical devices, consult a clinician before using rhythmic audio stimulation

Common questions

Why is there no sound?
Connect wired or Bluetooth headphones. The app is designed for headphone listening and may stop if audio output disconnects.

Does it work in the background?
Yes. Start a wave, lock your phone, and use the Lock Screen controls to pause, resume, or stop.

Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
